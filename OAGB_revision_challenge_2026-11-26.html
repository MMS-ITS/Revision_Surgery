Questions that should be answered before scoring or consenting

  1. Ulcer status: Current EGD, ulcer size/activity, stenosis and biopsy suitability.
  2. H. pylori: Test of cure after the second regimen, with a PPI-adjusted, clinician-managed schedule.
  3. Exact anatomy: Total small bowel, BP limb, alimentary limb, common channel, old GJ handling and ulcer-bearing tissue.
  4. Ulcer cure pathway: PPI dose/duration, H. pylori strategy, NSAID avoidance and objective healing endoscopy.
  5. Iron pathway: Ferritin, TSAT, CBC and a post-op replacement and monitoring schedule.
  6. Safeties: Leak testing, drain policy, VTE prophylaxis, ICU/interventional endoscopy access and rescue thresholds.
  7. Hiatus and gallbladder: Measured hernia, cruroplasty/mesh policy and cholecystectomy rationale.
  8. Experience: Surgeon’s exact-operation denominator and leak, bleeding, reoperation, readmission and recurrent ulcer rates.
  9. Aftercare: Name of clinician in Bangladesh or destination, local stay, fit-to-fly, emergency escalation and complication coverage.
  10. Written consent: Single operation sheet showing planned and conditional steps, alternatives and fallback procedures.

Medical decision-support disclaimer

This report is not medical advice, diagnosis or a recommendation to undergo surgery.
