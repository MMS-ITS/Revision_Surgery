Pros (Strengths)

  • Protects Lesser Curvature Blood Supply: Correctly explains why Highly Selective Vagotomy (HSV) is dangerous post-OAGB (would strip the left gastric artery arcade supplying the pouch) and uses safe Truncal Vagotomy instead.
